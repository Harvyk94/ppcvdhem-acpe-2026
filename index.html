Analysis scope and source

245 male, non-smoking profiles aged 40 without baseline diabetes; 7 LDL-C × 7 SBP × 5 Lp(a) levels. 10,000 people per arm; 450 cycles of 0.1 years; 5% discounting. Up to three active ingredients, common initiation ages 40–80 in five-year steps, and fixed regimens without escalation. Spironolactone is absent from the catalogue. Both modes share cardiovascular efficacy, catalogue, interaction exclusions, monitoring, population and simulation stream; only ADE application differs.

Each mode selects the highest NMB strategy from the shared candidate union. Differences between these reoptimised strategies include changes in treatment as well as ADE consequences; they are not a direct ADE burden estimate for a single unchanged regimen. ICERs compare with no medication, not successive options on an efficiency frontier. These are conditional model results; global optimality and external clinical validity have not been established.

Source: completed v3 campaign, all_comparisons.json; 490 verified winner replays. Data verification and source hashes: verification record. Results version: managed-event ADE v3, 28 October 2026. Analysis prepared for ACPE 2026.
